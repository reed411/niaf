  • Rounds 1, 3, 4: Winning Team = 2 pts each player. Individual Net: 1st=4, 2nd=3, 3rd=2, 4th=1.

  • Round 5 (Final Round): No team points. Individual Net: 1st=6, 2nd=4, 3rd=2, 4th=0.
  • Overall 5-Round Low Net Bonus: Awarded automatically at the end: 1st=6, 2nd=4, 3rd=2, 4th=0.
